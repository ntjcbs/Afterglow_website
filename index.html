Afterglow

Music Visualizer for Mac

Play anything, from Spotify to vinyl, and Afterglow turns the sound into stunning visuals. Thousands of looks, and you're fully in control.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Free · Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14.2 or later

Groove out

Afterglow listens to whatever's playing and moves with the music.

Thousands of looks

Mix shapes, palettes, effects and backgrounds. Afterglow morphs from one look to the next on its own, or you choose. Color studio fine-tunes any palette.

Take advantage of Apple displays

HDR highlights up to three times brighter than white, the full P3 color range, and up to 120 frames a second on ProMotion displays.

On your TV

Stream to your TV with AirPlay or Chromecast. HDR10 over AirPlay on compatible TVs.

Stage mode

Put the visualization on a TV or projector and keep the controls on your computer.

Private by design

No data collection. Free, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
