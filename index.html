Afterglow

Music Visualizer for Mac

Play anything, from Spotify to vinyl, and Afterglow turns the sound into stunning visuals. Thousands of looks, and you're fully in control.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Free · Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14.2 or later

It keeps time

Afterglow finds the tempo and beat of whatever's playing and moves with them. Between songs it can fade through black and arrive at a new look for the next track.

Thousands of looks

Mix shapes, palettes, effects and backgrounds. Afterglow morphs from one look to the next on its own, or you choose. Color studio fine-tunes any palette.

Made for Apple displays

HDR highlights up to three times brighter than white, the full Display P3 color range, and up to 120 frames a second on ProMotion displays.

On your TV

Send the visualization and the music to your TV with AirPlay or Chromecast, together in one stream. HDR10 over AirPlay on compatible TVs.

Stage mode

Put the visualization full screen on a TV or projector and keep the controls in a small window on your laptop.

Private by design

Afterglow hears your Mac's audio, never your screen. Audio is never saved. Free, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
